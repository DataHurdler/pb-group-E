# Module 03: Diagnose the Output

Put each output on screen, one at a time (11:02). Read the request aloud, show
the output, and ask: "Which slot was empty?" Have the room say it as a sentence:
"The output was ___, so the ___ slot was empty."

## 1

**Request:** "Write a reply to a resident who complained about a missed trash
pickup."

> Dear Valued Resident,
>
> The City of Brookfall is committed to excellence in service delivery and
> sincerely appreciates your patience as we continue to improve infrastructure
> and services across our community. Your feedback is important to us, and we
> are always working to serve you better. Please do not hesitate to reach out
> with any further concerns.
>
> Sincerely, The City of Brookfall

**Empty slot: Context.** No street, no date, no service-request process, no
facts, so it wrote generic reassurance. Fix: paste the complaint and the facts
(route, pickup day, what happens next).

## 2

**Request:** "When is the telework agreement renewed?" (policy pasted)

> Telework at the City of Brookfall is governed by Administrative Policy AP-14,
> which took effect on July 1, 2025, and is owned by the Human Resources
> Department. The policy establishes the conditions under which employees may
> perform some of their work from an approved location other than their City
> worksite. To be eligible, an employee must have completed six months of
> continuous service, must have a most recent performance evaluation of "Meets
> Expectations" or higher, and must hold a position that the department director
> has designated as telework-eligible…
>
> *(four more paragraphs)*
>
> …In summary, Telework Agreements expire each December 31 and must be renewed
> in January.

**Empty slot: Constraints.** It's correct but buries a one-line answer under
four paragraphs. Fix: "Answer in one sentence, then cite the section."

## 3

**Request:** "Explain who is eligible for telework, for supervisors to post in
the break room." (policy pasted)

> Under the City's telework policy, employees may request telework once they
> have completed six months of continuous service, provided their most recent
> evaluation is rated Meets Expectations or higher and their position has been
> designated telework-eligible by the department director, although positions
> involving in-person public contact, operation of City vehicles or equipment,
> or on-site supervision of field staff are not eligible, and regular telework
> is limited to two days per week with availability required from 9:00 to 3:00.

**Empty slot: Format.** Accurate, but one 80-word sentence is unusable on a
break-room wall. Fix: "A one-page notice: a headline, then a three-row table:
Who can, Who can't, The rules."
