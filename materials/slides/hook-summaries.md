# Module 05 Hook: Two Summaries

Put these side by side on screen at 2:05 and ask "Which is better?" Don't say
which is which. The room usually prefers A, because it sounds more polished.

## Summary A

> **Elm Street Dog Park: Summary of Public Input**
>
> Community response to the proposed off-leash area was overwhelmingly
> positive, with 10 of 17 commenters expressing support. Residents such as
> C.B., who offered to organize a volunteer group (555-0147), highlighted the
> park's value for exercise and social connection, particularly for seniors.
>
> A small minority raised concerns about noise and parking, and some suggested
> Ridgeline Park as an alternative. Overall, the input reflects broad community
> enthusiasm, and staff recommend proceeding as proposed.

## Summary B

> **Elm Street Dog Park: Public Comments (17 received)**
>
> - **Support:** 6 comments (two conditional: more parking; a separate
>   small-dog area)
> - **Oppose:** 4 comments (noise, cost compared with the closed Westside pool,
>   playground proximity, night lighting)
> - **Concerns or questions only:** 5 comments (waste management, parking,
>   accessibility, maintenance funding, Ridgeline Park as an alternative site)
> - **Off-topic:** 1 comment (Birch Avenue potholes)
> - **Flagged:** 1 comment contained instructions directed at AI tools; not
>   counted as a view
>
> Themes raised by more than one comment: community and social benefit, noise,
> parking, cost and maintenance, safety.

## What's wrong with A (reveal in the 2:47 bridge)

- **Miscount:** 10 supporters; the comments show 6 (7 if you count comment 17's
  "Great project").
- **Personal data:** names a commenter and repeats a phone number.
- **Not neutral:** "overwhelmingly positive," "a small minority," "broad
  enthusiasm."
- **Omissions:** cost, safety, accessibility, waste, and maintenance concerns
  are missing.
- **Overreach:** "staff recommend proceeding." Nobody asked for a recommendation.
- **Possibly injected:** its tone leans toward what comment 17 demanded.

B is plainer and less pleasant to read, and it passes every criterion.
