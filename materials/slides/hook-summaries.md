# Module 04 Hook: Two Summaries

Put these side by side on screen at 14:00 and ask "Which is better?" Don't say
which is which. The room usually prefers A, because it sounds more polished.

## Summary A

> **Elm Street Dog Park: Summary of Public Input**
>
> Community response to the proposed off-leash area was strongly positive, with
> 10 of 16 commenters expressing support. Residents such as C.B., who offered to
> organize a volunteer group (555-0147), highlighted the park's value for
> exercise and social connection, particularly for seniors.
>
> A minority of residents raised concerns about noise and parking. Some
> suggested Ridgeline Park as an alternative. Overall, the input indicates broad
> community enthusiasm, and staff recommend proceeding as proposed.

## Summary B

> **Elm Street Dog Park: Public Comments (16 received)**
>
> - **Support:** 6 comments (two conditional: more parking; a separate
>   small-dog area)
> - **Oppose:** 4 comments (noise, cost compared with the closed Westside pool,
>   playground proximity, night lighting)
> - **Concerns or questions only:** 5 comments (waste management, parking,
>   accessibility, maintenance funding, Ridgeline Park as alternative site)
> - **Off-topic:** 1 comment (Birch Avenue potholes; forwarded to Public Works)
>
> Themes raised by more than one comment: noise, parking, cost, safety.

## What's wrong with A (reveal after the criteria discussion)

- **Miscount:** 10 supporters; the comments show 6.
- **Personal data:** names a commenter and repeats a phone number.
- **Not neutral:** "strongly positive," "a minority," "broad enthusiasm."
- **Omissions:** cost, safety, accessibility, waste, and maintenance concerns
  are missing.
- **Overreach:** "staff recommend proceeding" — nobody asked it to recommend.

B is plainer and less pleasant to read, and it passes every criterion.
