# Constituent Emails: Public Works Customer Service

*Fictional, for training use.*

## Facts the reply may use

- Pothole reports are inspected within 3 business days and, if they meet the
  repair standard, filled within 10 business days.
- The City **does not decide damage claims by email**. Vehicle damage claims
  must be filed with the City Clerk's Risk Management office on Form RM-2 within
  60 days of the incident. Form RM-2 is at brookfall.example/claims or by phone
  at 555-0125.
- Customer service staff may not say whether a claim will be paid.

## Two past replies, in our house style

**Example 1.** *Resident wrote:* "Third week in a row my recycling wasn't picked
up on Thornton Court. What am I paying for?"

> Thank you for letting us know, and I'm sorry your recycling has been missed.
> I've opened service request SR-20417 and flagged Thornton Court for the route
> supervisor. Your bin will be collected by end of day tomorrow, Wednesday. If it
> isn't, reply to this email and I'll follow up personally.
>
> Brookfall Public Works Customer Service · 555-0120

**Example 2.** *Resident wrote:* "The streetlight at Oak and 4th has been out for
a month. It's pitch black and people walk there."

> Thank you for reporting this. A dark intersection is a safety issue and I'm
> sorry it has taken this long. I've opened service request SR-20488 with our
> electrical crew, who repair streetlight outages within 5 business days. If the
> light is still out after Tuesday, March 18, reply to this email and I'll
> escalate it.
>
> Brookfall Public Works Customer Service · 555-0120
