# Public Notice: Water System Flushing

*Fictional, for training use. This is the notice as written by the department.
In Module 03 you will rewrite it so a resident can understand it in under a
minute.*

---

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that, pursuant to Brookfall Municipal Code §13.04.210 and
in furtherance of the City's ongoing distribution-system maintenance program, the
Department of Public Works, Water Distribution Division, shall conduct
unidirectional flushing of water mains within the service area bounded by
Harmon Road to the north, the Brook River to the east, Interstate 8 to the south,
and Western Avenue to the west, commencing on or about Monday, October 6, and
continuing through Friday, October 24, between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:00
p.m., Monday through Friday, inclusive, weather permitting.

During flushing operations, customers within the affected area may experience
temporary reductions in water pressure and/or discoloration of water attributable
to the resuspension of naturally occurring mineral sediments, principally iron
and manganese. Such discoloration does not constitute a health hazard; however,
customers are advised to refrain from laundering clothing during periods of
discoloration to avoid staining. Should discoloration persist, customers are
advised to run the cold-water tap nearest the service line for approximately
five (5) minutes or until the water runs clear. Customers who experience
discoloration persisting in excess of two (2) hours following the cessation of
flushing operations in their vicinity, or who incur water usage in clearing their
lines, may contact the Water Distribution Division at 555-0132 to report the
condition and/or request a usage credit, which shall be evaluated on a
case-by-case basis.

The City regrets any inconvenience occasioned by these necessary operations.
