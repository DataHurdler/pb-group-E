# City of Brookfall Administrative Policy AP-14: Telework

*Fictional, for training use. Effective July 1, 2025. Owner: Human Resources Department.*

**1. Purpose.** This policy sets the conditions under which City employees may
perform some of their work from an approved location other than their City
worksite.

**2. Eligibility.** An employee may request telework if all of the following
are true:
(a) the employee has completed six (6) months of continuous City service;
(b) the employee's most recent performance evaluation is rated "Meets
Expectations" or higher; and
(c) the employee's position has been designated telework-eligible by the
department director.
Positions that require in-person contact with the public, operation of City
vehicles or equipment, or on-site supervision of field staff are not
telework-eligible.

**3. Schedule.** Regular telework is limited to a maximum of two (2) days per
work week. Teleworking employees must be available by phone and email during
core hours, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Every telework arrangement requires a written
Telework Agreement signed by the employee and supervisor. Agreements expire each
December 31 and must be renewed in January.

**4. Equipment and data.** The City provides a City-issued laptop. The employee
is responsible for a reliable internet connection at the telework location. The
City does not reimburse home internet, phone, or utility costs. City data may be
stored only on City-issued devices or City-approved cloud storage.

**5. Location.** Telework must be performed within the state. Telework from
outside the state requires advance written approval from the Human Resources
Director and is limited to ten (10) working days per calendar year.

**6. Ending an arrangement.** Either the employee or the supervisor may end a
Telework Agreement with ten (10) working days' written notice. The City may end
an arrangement immediately for performance concerns or operational need.

**7. Questions.** Contact Human Resources at 555-0110 or hr@brookfall.example.
