# Memo: Summary of the New Telework Policy

*This memo was "written by an AI" from the telework policy. Some of it is right
and some of it is wrong. Your job in Activity 01 is to find out which.*

---

**To:** All Parks and Recreation Staff
**From:** Department Administration
**Re:** What the new telework policy (AP-14) means for you

Good news: the City's new telework policy took effect July 1, 2025, and many of
you are now eligible to work from home part of the week. Here is what you need
to know.

**Who can telework.** Any employee who has completed three months of City
service, has a satisfactory performance rating, and works in a position the
director has designated as telework-eligible may apply. Positions that involve
operating City vehicles or working directly with the public are not eligible.

**How often.** You may telework up to three days per week. While teleworking you
must be reachable by phone and email during core hours, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

**Paperwork.** You and your supervisor will sign a Telework Agreement. Agreements
are valid for two years and then must be renewed.

**Equipment.** The City will issue you a laptop. To help with costs, the City
will reimburse up to $40 per month for home internet service; submit receipts to
your supervisor monthly.

**Location.** Telework must be performed inside the state. Telework from outside
the state is not permitted under any circumstances.

**Legal basis.** These rules follow the telework provisions in Section 9.2 of the
Fair Labor Standards Act, which requires public employers to offer telework to
eligible staff.

**Ending telework.** Either you or your supervisor can end the arrangement with
ten working days' written notice.

Questions? Contact Human Resources at 555-0110.
