# Plan: Online Pothole Reporting, Launch in Three Weeks

*Fictional, for training use. Draft from the Public Works customer-service
supervisor to the director. Used in Module 04, Station F.*

---

**Goal.** Let residents report potholes online instead of by phone, starting
Monday, November 3.

**How it will work.**

1. A new page on brookfall.example with a form: location, description, an
   optional photo, and an optional email for updates.
2. Reports go to the existing customer-service inbox. Staff copy each one into
   the work-order system by hand, as they do with phone reports today.
3. Residents who give an email get an automatic "We received your report" reply.

**Staffing.** No new staff. Customer service currently takes about 40 pothole
calls a week. We expect online reports to replace most calls, so workload
should stay about the same.

**Communication.** A post on the City's social media accounts the week of
launch, and a line on the next utility bill (mails November 20).

**Cost.** None beyond staff time. IT says the form can be built with the
website's existing tools.

**Success.** Fewer phone calls about potholes by the end of November.
