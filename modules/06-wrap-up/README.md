# 06 Wrap-up

Status: ready
Time: 15:40–16:00 (20 min) · Talk 4 / do 16
Outcomes: all (checked)

## Purpose

Measure the change since 9:00, turn the day into one concrete Monday action, and
close the parking lot.

## Timed outline

| Min | Segment | Format | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Two share-outs | Volunteers | Each shows their template on screen: the task, the technique they chose and why, and what the partner test changed |
| 5–10 | Post-check | Individual | Same [8 questions](../../assessment/pre-post-check.md) as the morning |
| 10–14 | Monday plan | Individual | On an index card they keep: **one task** I'll use my template on, **one habit** I'll keep, **one rule** I won't break |
| 14–18 | Close | Instructor | Resources, the follow-up email, parking-lot answers (or when they'll get them), where to go next |
| 18–20 | Feedback | Individual | [Feedback form](../../assessment/feedback-form.md) |

## Closing talking points (4 min)

- Return to the 9:00 hook: "You can now say exactly why the second prompt
  worked."
- The three habits: **new task, new chat**; **change one thing at a time**;
  **you sign it, you own it**.
- What's in the follow-up email: all handouts, the prompt library, the Brookfall
  files for practice, and answers to the parking lot.
- Where to go next: the agency's AI policy owner, any internal user community,
  and practising with their template on a real (approved-data) task this week.

## Instructor notes

- Don't run past 4:00. If behind, drop share-outs first; the post-check and
  feedback form must happen in the room or response rates collapse.
- Answer only parking-lot items you can answer accurately. Promise the rest in
  writing.
