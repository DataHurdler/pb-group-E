## Closing talking points (4 min)

- Back to the 8:30 demo: "You can now say exactly why the second prompt
  worked: slots, a source, constraints, a check."
- Three habits: **new task, new chat**; **change one thing at a time**; **you
  sign it, you own it**.
- Parking-lot answers you can give accurately now; the rest by email.
- What's in the follow-up email: every handout, the prompt library, the
  Brookfall files for practice, parking-lot answers, and in 30 days a
  two-question check-in on your Monday card.
- Where this goes next in the series: *Introduction to AI and Machine Learning*
  (how the models work and when they fit), *AI Ethics and Governance* (the
  organizational side of today's safe-use rules), *Building an AI Agent* (an
  agent's instructions are a prompt), and *Data Analytics in Excel*.

## Instructor notes

- Don't run past 4:00. If behind, shorten the close to one minute; the
  post-check and feedback must happen in the room or response rates collapse.
- Answer only parking-lot items you can answer accurately. Promise the rest in
  writing, and send it.
