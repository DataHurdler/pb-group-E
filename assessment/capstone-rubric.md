# Capstone Rubric

Status: ready
Used: Module 05 partner test · Outcomes: LO2–LO5

Score the **template**, not the writing it produced and not the author's job
knowledge. 0 = missing, 1 = partly there, 2 = solid. A template scoring 7 or
more is ready to use on Monday.

| Row | What to look for | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. CRAFT complete** (LO3) | All five parts present and specific | Two or more parts missing | All present, one or two vague ("make it good") | All five specific enough that two people would read them the same way |
| **2. Technique fit** (LO4) | A technique from Module 03 that fits this task's problem | None | One used, but not clearly needed | One or more chosen for a reason the author can state |
| **3. Reusable** (LO5) | Brackets for what changes; runs without asking the author | Partner had to ask several questions | Partner asked one question, or guessed at a bracket | Partner filled it in and ran it unaided |
| **4. Safe and verifiable** (LO2, LO5) | "Before you paste" line; Test part flags assumptions; "before you send" checklist | None of the three | One or two of the three | All three, and the send checklist names the specific facts to check |
| **5. Partner result** (LO5) | The output from the partner's run | Not usable | Usable after substantial edits | Usable as a first draft with minor edits |
