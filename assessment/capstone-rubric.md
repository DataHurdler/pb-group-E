# Capstone Rubric

Status: ready
Used: Module 06 cold partner test · Objectives: LO2–LO5, SU

Score the **template**, not the writing it produced, and not the author's job
knowledge. **You are not grading anyone as a writer or a subject expert.**

**Not yet** = missing · **Workable** = there, but vague or partial ·
**Strong** = someone else could rely on it. Workable or better on all five
rows means it's ready for Monday.

| Row | Look for | Not yet | Workable | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Clear and structured** (LO2) | The slots the task needs | Task unclear, or Context/Audience missing | All needed slots present; one or two vague ("make it good") | Every slot specific enough that two people would read it the same way |
| **2. Technique fit** (LO3) | A Module 04 technique that fixes a failure the author saw | None | One used, but not clearly needed | Chosen for a failure the author can name |
| **3. Tested** (LO4) | The author's test note, plus your own run | Run once, or no test note | Run on two inputs, or improved one change at a time | Run on a normal and a hard or probe input; changes logged; your run agreed |
| **4. Reusable and efficient** (LO5) | Header, brackets, runs cold | Partner had to ask several questions | Partner asked one question or guessed at a bracket | Partner filled it in and ran it unaided; header says where *not* to use it |
| **5. Safe and verifiable** (SU) | Before-you-paste line, flag line, before-you-send checklist | None of the three | One or two of the three | All three, and the send checklist names the specific facts to check |
