# Activity 5: Capstone, Your Reusable Prompt

Module 05 · 36 min · Individual, then pairs · LO3, LO4, LO5, LO2

## Goal

Turn your Activity 2B prompt into a template that you, or a coworker, could
reuse next month and get a usable first draft.

## Requirements

Your template must have:

1. **All five CRAFT parts.**
2. **At least one technique** from this afternoon (ground it, show it, break it
   down, let it ask), chosen because it fits your task.
3. **`[BRACKETS]`** for everything that changes each time, so someone else can
   fill them in without asking you.
4. **A "before you paste" line** at the top, saying what must be removed or
   checked first.
5. **A Test part** that makes the AI flag assumptions or missing information.
6. **A "before you send" checklist** at the bottom: the 2–4 things *you* will
   verify by hand every time.

## Steps

1. **(15 min) Build it.** Start from your 2B prompt and the
   [prompt library](../handouts/prompt-library.md). Run it at least once with
   your made-up material.
2. **(12 min) Partner test.** Swap templates with a partner. Without help from
   its author:
   - Fill in the brackets with made-up details
   - Run it in a new chat
   - Score it with the [capstone rubric](../assessment/capstone-rubric.md)
   - Tell the author **one thing to keep and one thing to change**
3. **(7 min) Revise** based on what your partner found. Run it once more.
4. **(2 min) Save it** somewhere you'll find it on Monday: a document, a note,
   or an email to yourself.

**You're done when** a partner has run your template without asking you a
question, and you've saved the revised version.

## Template skeleton

```
BEFORE YOU PASTE: remove [names / account numbers / …]. Check [ … ].

[CONTEXT: who I am, the situation, the source]
[REQUEST: one verb, one job]
[AUDIENCE: who reads it, what they know, tone, reading level]
[FORMAT: length, structure, what to keep exactly]
[TEST: "Use only … If something is missing, say … List anything you assumed."]

<source>
[PASTE]
</source>

BEFORE YOU SEND, I CHECK:
- [ ]
- [ ]
- [ ]
```
