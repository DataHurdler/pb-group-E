# Activity 2A: Rescue a Weak Prompt

Module 02 · 25 min · Pairs · LO3

## Goal

Turn two weak prompts into CRAFT prompts, run both versions, and see what
changed.

## You need

- [../handouts/craft-cheat-sheet.md](../handouts/craft-cheat-sheet.md)
- Your AI tool, and the Brookfall files in [../materials/data/](../materials/data/)

## The weak prompts

| # | Weak prompt | Paste with it | The real need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Make this easier to read." | [hydrant-notice.md](../materials/data/hydrant-notice.md) | A version for the city website that residents, some with limited English, can read on a phone |
| 2 | "Summarize these notes." | [meeting-notes.md](../materials/data/meeting-notes.md) | An action list the chair can send to staff today: who does what by when |
| 3 | "Write an email about the new telework policy." | *nothing* | An announcement to Parks staff, from their director, that gets the facts right ([telework-policy.md](../materials/data/telework-policy.md)) |

## Steps

Do **#1**, then pick #2 or #3.

1. **(2 min) Run the weak prompt exactly as written,** in a new chat. Save the
   result.
2. **(7 min) Write a CRAFT version** together. Fill all five parts. Use the
   "real need" column, and decide anything it doesn't tell you.
3. **(3 min) Run it in a new chat.** Compare it with the first result side by
   side. Which part of CRAFT made the biggest difference?
4. **(13 min) Repeat with your second prompt.**

**You're done when** you have a weak and a CRAFT result for two prompts, and
can name the part of CRAFT that helped most for each.

## Before you paste

Everything in this activity is fictional and Green. Say it out loud anyway; it's
the habit we're building.

## If you finish early

Ask the AI to critique your CRAFT prompt: "What's missing from this prompt that
would help you do a better job?" Is its advice any good?
