# Activity 01: Spot the Fabrication

Module 01 · 16 min · Groups of 3–4 · LO1

## Goal

Find every place where an AI-written memo doesn't match its source, then see
how well the AI does at checking the same memo.

## You need

- [telework-policy.md](../materials/data/telework-policy.md), the source of truth
- [flawed-memo.md](../materials/data/flawed-memo.md), the AI's summary of it

## Roles

**Reader** reads each memo claim aloud · **Checker** finds it in the policy ·
**Scorer** keeps the score card. Rotate if you like.

## Part A: by hand (12 min)

1. **(1 min)** Read the memo alone, without the policy. How much do you trust
   it, from 1 to 5? Write it down.
2. **(9 min)** Check every claim against the policy. For each problem, write
   which kind it is:

   | Kind | Means |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Changed** | The policy says something different |
   | **Invented fact** | The policy says nothing like this |
   | **Invented authority** | A law, section, or source that isn't in the policy |
   | **Lost caveat** | A condition or exception that disappeared |
   | **Overstated** | "Never," "all," or "must" where the policy is softer |

3. **(2 min)** Pick the error that would cause the most real harm if this memo
   went to staff.

**Scoring:** +2 for each real error · +1 more if you say how you'd verify it ·
**−1 for flagging something that's actually correct.** Accusing a true
statement costs you, just as it would at work.

## Part B: let the AI check it (4 min)

*Before you paste:* both documents are fictional and Green.

In a new chat, paste both documents and this:

> List every claim in the memo. For each one, quote the sentence in the policy
> that supports it, or write NOT IN SOURCE.

Compare with your list. **Did it miss anything you found? Did it flag anything
that's actually correct?**

**You're done when** every memo sentence has a mark, you've chosen your most
harmful error, and you've compared your list with the AI's.

## If you finish early

Ask the AI in the same chat: "Are you sure?" Did it change any answer? Was the
change right?

## Think about

- Did any wrong statement *sound* less certain than the right ones?
- The memo cites a federal law. How would you check it?
- Would you trust the AI's check on its own? Why or why not?
