# Activity 02: Safe to Paste?

Module 02 · 14 min · Groups, then pairs · SU

## Goal

Agree which of twelve things may go into an approved AI tool, then rescue a
risky prompt so the task can still be done safely.

## You need

- [../handouts/safe-to-paste.md](../handouts/safe-to-paste.md)
- The twelve cards below (printed and cut, or on screen)

## Part A: card sort (8 min, groups)

1. **(5 min)** Sort each card into 🟢 Green, 🟡 Yellow, or 🔴 Red, assuming
   you're in your agency's approved tool with your work account. Disagreement is
   fine; note it.
2. **(2 min)** For every Red card: could you still get help with the task by
   removing or making up details? How?
3. **(1 min)** Pick the card your group argued about most.

### Cards

1. The adopted city budget, as published on the city website
2. A resident's complaint email, including their name, home address, and phone number
3. Your draft agenda for next week's internal staff meeting
4. A performance evaluation you're writing for someone you supervise, with the name removed
5. A job posting HR will publish next week
6. Vendor bid prices received before the contract is awarded
7. A made-up scenario: "a resident whose tire was damaged by a pothole"
8. A screenshot of your case-management system showing a list of clients
9. The text of a state statute, copied from the legislature's website
10. Your building's security procedures and door-access schedule
11. Public comments on a proposal, which include some commenters' phone numbers
12. A paragraph you wrote describing your program for this year's annual report

## Part B: clean this prompt (6 min, pairs)

A colleague wrote this prompt. **Don't run it.** Rewrite it so the AI can still
do the job, with nothing that identifies anyone.

> Write a reply to Jordan Ellis (jordan.ellis@email.example, 555-0163, 418
> Cedar Lane, water account W-48213). She's upset about a $96 late fee on her water
> bill. She says she was in the hospital for her son's asthma in August and
> missed the due date. My supervisor Marisol Vega approved waiving the fee as a
> one-time courtesy. Apologize and tell her the fee is gone.

1. **(2 min)** Underline everything that identifies a person or is sensitive.
2. **(3 min)** Rewrite it. Decide: what does the AI actually *need* to write a
   good reply?
3. **(1 min)** Would the reply still be good? What will you add back by hand
   before you send it?

**You're done when** all twelve cards are sorted and you have a cleaned prompt
that still does the job.

## If you finish early

Run your cleaned prompt. Does the reply still work? What exactly will you add
back by hand before sending?
