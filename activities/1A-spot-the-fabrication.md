# Activity 1A: Spot the Fabrication

Module 01 · 15 min · Groups of 3–4 · LO1

## Goal

Find every place where an AI-written memo doesn't match its source, and notice
how convincing the wrong parts sound.

## You need

- [../materials/data/telework-policy.md](../materials/data/telework-policy.md), the source of truth
- [../materials/data/flawed-memo.md](../materials/data/flawed-memo.md), the AI's summary of it
- A pen, or a shared document

## Steps

1. **(2 min) Read the memo alone.** Don't look at the policy yet. Does it seem
   trustworthy? Mark your gut confidence from 1 to 5.
2. **(8 min) Check it against the policy as a group.** For each claim in the memo,
   mark it ✓ (matches), ✗ (wrong), or ? (not in the policy at all). Note the
   policy section.
3. **(3 min) Sort what you found** into three kinds:
   - **Changed:** the policy says something different
   - **Invented:** the policy says nothing like this
   - **Dropped:** the policy says something the memo leaves out
4. **(2 min) Pick the one error** that would cause the most real harm if this
   memo went out to staff. Be ready to say why.

**You're done when** every sentence in the memo has a mark and you've chosen your
most harmful error.

## Think about

- Did any wrong statement *sound* less certain than the right ones?
- Which error would you have missed if you hadn't had the policy beside you?
- The memo cites a federal law. How would you check it?
