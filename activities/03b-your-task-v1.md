# Activity 03b: Your Own Task, Version 1

Module 03 · 15 min · Individual · LO2, SU

## Goal

Write and run a first structured prompt for the task on your index card. You'll
add a technique after lunch, test it, and finish it as a reusable template at
3:05.

## Step 1: Confirm the task (2 min)

Something you do **at least monthly** that involves writing, summarizing,
rewriting, or organizing information. Good choices:

- Answering a common type of email or phone inquiry
- Turning notes into minutes or action items
- Writing a recurring report, notice, or announcement
- Rewriting technical or legal text for the public
- Preparing a briefing for your supervisor

**Not today:** anything that decides something about a specific person
(eligibility, hiring, discipline, enforcement, benefits).

## Step 2: Before you paste (2 min)

What material would this task normally use? ____________________

Is any of it 🟡 Yellow or 🔴 Red? **Make up a realistic stand-in** for today:
same shape, fake details. Write it in a document now. You'll hand it to your
capstone partner at 3:25, so make it a full stand-in, not a sketch.
