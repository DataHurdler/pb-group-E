# Activity 1B: Safe to Paste?

Module 01 · 10 min · Groups of 3–4 · LO2

## Goal

Agree, as a group, which of twelve things may go into an approved AI tool.

## You need

- [../handouts/safe-to-paste.md](../handouts/safe-to-paste.md)
- The twelve cards below (printed and cut, or on screen)

## Steps

1. **(6 min) Sort each card** into 🟢 Green, 🟡 Yellow, or 🔴 Red, assuming
   you're using your agency's approved tool. Disagreement is fine; note it.
2. **(2 min) For every Red card, ask:** could you still get help from AI with
   this task by removing or making up details? How?
3. **(2 min) Pick the card your group argued about most.** You'll share it.

**You're done when** all twelve cards are sorted and you've picked one to share.

## Cards

1. The adopted city budget, as published on the city website
2. A resident's complaint email, including their name, home address, and phone number
3. Your draft agenda for next week's internal staff meeting
4. A performance evaluation you're writing for someone you supervise
5. A job posting HR will publish next week
6. Vendor bid prices received before the contract is awarded
7. A made-up scenario: "a resident whose tire was damaged by a pothole"
8. A screenshot of your case-management system showing a list of clients
9. The text of a state statute, copied from the legislature's website
10. Your building's security procedures and door-access schedule
11. Public comments on a proposal, which include some commenters' phone numbers
12. A paragraph you wrote describing your program for this year's annual report
